	•	Name:

	•	Origin:
	•	Mythic & Geographic Roots: Primarily introduced by Plato in his dialogues Timaeus and Critias, describing an advanced island civilization that was destroyed by catastrophe. Modern speculation places its location in various oceanic regions (Atlantic, Mediterranean, Caribbean). Indigenous flood myths from diverse cultures (e.g., Mesoamerican, Native American, Aboriginal) often resonate with a great cataclysm that destroyed an earlier world.
