Entry 22: Blood Rites
	•	Name:
	•	Common: Sacred Blood Offerings, Menstrual Ceremonies, Rites of Passage (Blood).
	•	Esoteric: Rites of Rebirth, Primordial Waters of Life, The Red Current, Lunar Offering, Initiation by Blood, Earth's Nectar.
	•	Origin:
	•	Mythic & Geographic Roots: Foundational and ancient across virtually all indigenous, tribal, and early goddess-worshipping cultures, long predating patriarchal suppression. Primarily associated with the sacredness of menstrual blood as a powerful life-giving and regenerative force, but also includes blood from birth, initiation, or sacrifice (e.g., animal, symbolic). Evident in:
	•	Ancient Matriarchal Societies: Where menstrual cycles were honored as sacred, women were seen as direct conduits of divine life force.
	•	Indigenous Traditions: Many Native American, African, and Aboriginal cultures have specific ceremonies around puberty, menstruation, birth, and death involving blood as a sacred element.
	•	Fertility Cults: Blood was seen as vital for fertile lands and prosperous communities.
	•	Shamanic Practices: Blood offerings or ingestion (symbolic or literal) for vision, healing, or communion with spirits.
	•	Early Goddess Worship: Blood was often revered as the purest essence of the Great Mother's creative power.
	•	The practice contrasts sharply with later Abrahamic and patriarchal traditions which often demonized or ritualistically excluded menstrual blood.
