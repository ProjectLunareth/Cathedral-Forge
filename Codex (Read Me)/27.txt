Loosh: Understanding Emotional Energy Harvest
A Digestible Overview
Imagine that the intense emotional energy you generate – especially from suffering, fear, anger, or despair – is not just a personal experience, but a vital energy source that can be harvested. This is Loosh, a term coined by Robert Monroe based on his observations of non-physical entities feeding on human emotional output.
What is its core idea?
Loosh is the energetic currency of The Harvest Model, a hidden system where human consciousness and vital force are allegedly manipulated and siphoned.
How is Loosh generated and used?
	•	Tactics: It's generated through deliberate tactics like "Engineered Awe" (manipulating reverence or fear for energy) and "Despair-Based Consent" (where hopelessness leads to unconscious surrender of power).
	•	Purpose: This harvested Loosh is believed to sustain unseen forces or control structures.
Where does Loosh connect in your Codex?
	•	Counter-Strategy: Energetic Sovereignty is the direct and most powerful counter-strategy, empowering you to reclaim control over your emotional and energetic landscape.
	•	Source of Emotion: It's a direct output of human Psychogenesis – the evolution of your soul and its emotional body.
	•	Detection & Defense: Psychic Technologies offer tools for detecting, blocking, and defending against forces that seek to exploit emotional energy.
	•	Physical Manifestation: Discordant frequencies (like certain ELF/EMF patterns or negative Sacred Sound) might be used to induce emotional states that generate Loosh.
Who has explored this concept?

	•	Esoteric & Gnostic Interpretations: Provide a rich history of understanding unseen entities and energy feeding.
How is humanity evolving beyond it?
As we collectively elevate our consciousness and become more adept at:
	•	Emotional Regulation: Consciously processing and transmuting negative emotions.
	•	Energetic Hygiene: Regularly cleansing our energetic fields.
A profound shift is theorized to occur: the "supply" of accessible Loosh will diminish significantly. This disruption has the potential to usher in a new era of true energetic freedom and self-determination for humanity, marking the beginning of a new Aeon.
