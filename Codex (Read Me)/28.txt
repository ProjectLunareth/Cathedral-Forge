Engineered Awe: The Illusion of Control
A Digestible Overview
Imagine a dazzling spectacle, a profound revelation, or a charismatic figure designed to overwhelm your senses and bypass your critical thinking, inducing deep reverence, worship, or fear. This is Engineered Awe – a sophisticated tactic within the Harvest Model to generate and siphon your emotional energy.

It aims to:
	•	Generate "Loosh": By inducing intense emotional states (adoration, fear-reverence), it harvests this energy for unseen forces.
	•	Gain Consent: It can subtly lead to Despair-Based Consent, where individuals, overwhelmed by the spectacle, unconsciously surrender their autonomy.
	•	Control Perception: It creates an illusion of profound truth or divine authority to manipulate collective consciousness.
