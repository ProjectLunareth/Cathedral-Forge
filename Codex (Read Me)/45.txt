Psychic Technologies: Unlocking Your Latent Abilities
A Digestible Overview
Imagine that within every human being lies a vast reservoir of untapped abilities – innate faculties like telepathy, clairvoyance, and energy manipulation, which science is just beginning to understand. These are Psychic Technologies – inherent human capabilities that, while often dormant or suppressed, are now beginning to awaken collectively.
What is their core essence?
They are:
	•	Innate Human Faculties: Not supernatural, but natural extensions of consciousness.
	•	Tools of Perception: Allowing you to perceive beyond ordinary physical senses.
	•	Tools of Influence: Enabling you to interact with energy and reality through conscious intent.
Where do Psychic Technologies connect in your Codex?
	•	Path to Development: Developing these abilities is a natural outcome and advanced stage of The Corpus of Ascent, cultivated through practices like Inner Climbing Systems.
	•	Ultimate Purpose: They are crucial for achieving Energetic Sovereignty, empowering you to harness your inner power and live in harmony with your true nature.
	•	Countering Manipulation: They enable you to detect and defend against the manipulations of The Harvest Model, including Loosh extraction, Engineered Awe, and Despair-Based Consent.
	•	Energetic Mechanics: Their function relies on Vibrational Attunement, allowing you to tune into subtle frequencies.
	•	Hidden Knowledge: Their existence and full potential are often considered Forbidden Knowledge and Suppressed Wisdom, as they threaten established power structures.
	•	Future Potential: They are commonly utilized by Higher Civilizations, providing a template for humanity's own future.
Who has explored these abilities?
	•	Governmental Research: Works on remote viewing from pioneers like Ingo Swann and Joseph McMoneagle (e.g., Stanford Research Institute's CIA Stargate Project).
	•	Consciousness Research: Scientific investigations into the mind's capabilities, like those by Dean Radin.
	•	Esoteric Texts: Ancient wisdom traditions describing psi abilities and energy healing modalities.
How is humanity evolving through Psychic Technologies?
As humanity's collective consciousness rises (Psychogenesis):
	•	Widespread Awakening: The emergence of latent Psychic Technologies is accelerating.
	•	Dismantling Control: This will cause the old paradigms of manipulation and control to crumble.
	•	New Era: It will usher in an Aeon of Energetic Sovereignty, characterized by transparency, direct perception, and conscious co-creation.
Why is this crucial for your Energetic Sovereignty?
Developing your Psychic Technologies empowers you to:
	•	Discern Reality: See through illusions and understand subtle energetic influences.
	•	Self-Defense: Actively create energetic shields and project high-vibrational intentions.
	•	Co-Create: Fully participate in shaping a brighter, more compassionate future, one where individual and collective evolution are intertwined, and the boundaries between self and world are transcended.
This extraordinary journey invites you to unlock the secrets of the human experience and unleash your full potential, giving rise to a brighter, more enlightened future for all.
