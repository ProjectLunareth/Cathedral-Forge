	•	Specific Technique: Basic Sigil Construction (Chaos Magic Method):
	1.	Formulate Desire: Write down your clear, concise intention in a positive statement (e.g., "I easily attract abundance").
	2.	Condense Letters: Remove all repeating letters from the statement (e.g., "I E A S L Y A T R C B U N D").
	3.	Create Glyph: Arrange the remaining letters into a single, aesthetically pleasing, abstract symbol. You can combine lines, curves, and parts of letters. The goal is to make it look like a unique design, not immediately recognizable as its constituent letters. This becomes your Personal Sigil.
	4.	Charge the Sigil: Focus intensely on the sigil. You can do this by staring at it, tracing it with your finger, chanting the intention repeatedly, or building up a strong emotional state (e.g., excitement, passion) and then "firing" the intention into the sigil at the peak of that state. The point is to bypass the conscious mind, impressing it upon the subconscious.
	5.	Forget & Release: Once charged, actively forget the sigil and its intention. Put it away, burn it, dispose of it—whatever feels like a release. This allows the subconscious mind to work on manifesting the intention without conscious interference or doubt.
