	•	Mythic & Geographic Roots: Universal across virtually all creation myths and spiritual traditions. In Vedic/Hinduism, creation emerges from Nada Brahma (Sound as God/Brahman), with "Om" as the primordial sound. Ancient Egypt believed creation was spoken into being by Thoth. In Abrahamic traditions, the universe was created through "the Word" (Logos). Indigenous cultures across the globe (e.g., Australian Aboriginal "Dreamtime" songs, Native American chanting) use sacred sound for healing, invoking spirits, and connecting with creation. Pythagoreanism saw the universe as a vast musical scale, reflecting celestial harmonies.
